Process

From script to visuals in four steps

01

Paste your script

Write the words you will speak. We generate one full narration track first, then use its length to time every scene.

02

Choose narration speed

Pick how fast the voice reads your script (0.25 = slower, 1.0 = normal). This is locked when you create the project — you cannot change or regenerate the voice later.

03

Set your scene pace

Choose how many images per minute for the opening 5 minutes, then a separate rate for the rest — faster pace early, slower later.

04

Cinematic scene images

Each scene gets a matching Tatterveil-style image at your chosen resolution and quality — consistent palette, atmosphere, and 16:9 framing.

How timing works

Before generation, we show a rough preview based on your script length and narration speed. The real video length comes from the generated narration audio — scene timestamps always match that recording.

Scene count is split into two segments: the opening 5 minutes (higher image rate for visual variety) and the rest of the video (lower rate for a more measured pace). You control both rates with the sliders.

The maximum is 8 scenes/min because above that each image would show for under 8 seconds — too brief to register visually.
